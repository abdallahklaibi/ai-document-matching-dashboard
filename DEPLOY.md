# Management demo deployment checklist

## Before the meeting
- Put your company name/logo into the dashboard header if desired.
- Test with a non-sensitive representative PDF and Excel file.
- Check that the Excel phone-number column is detected correctly.
- Configure the Anthropic API key in Streamlit Secrets if the PDF is scanned/handwritten.
- Run one full test and download the resulting workbook.

## Live demo sequence
1. Open the permanent Streamlit link.
2. Upload the PDF and Excel.
3. Show that the phone column was detected.
4. Click **Run Monthly Analysis**.
5. Explain the KPI cards.
6. Filter the table to Medium/Low confidence to demonstrate human review.
7. Download the highlighted Excel report.
8. Open the **AI Audit** sheet to demonstrate traceability.

## Suggested management wording
"This workflow converts a repetitive monthly reconciliation task into a controlled AI-assisted process. It automatically identifies likely matches, separates uncertain cases for human review, and creates an auditable Excel output."
